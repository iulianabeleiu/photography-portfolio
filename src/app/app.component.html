Behind the camera

Technology by trade.
Photography by instinct.

Most days, I work in technology—thinking in systems, structure and precise solutions. Photography is where I let observation lead instead. The two practices meet in curiosity: looking closely, understanding how things connect, and finding the simplest frame that still holds the whole story.

This is an evolving journal of personal work, slow travel and commissioned stories. Every image is made with attention to natural light and honest moments.
